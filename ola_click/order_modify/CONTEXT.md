# Order modification ICM

Change an existing order, including cancellation, status, discounts, and payments.

| Stage | Handoff |
|---|---|
| [01 Extract](stages/01_extract/CONTEXT.md) | Checked operations or `stage01_intent.json`. |
| [02 Resolve and execute](stages/02_resolve_and_execute/CONTEXT.md) | `orientation.json`. |

For a full run, follow the stages in order. Enter Stage 02 directly when given a checked Stage 01 artifact for this ICM. For extraction-only work, stop at the Stage 01 handoff.
